Setting up a new user

How to create someone's login, give them a role, and decide what that role is allowed to see and do.

  • →
  • Give them a role

  • Set what the role can do

Before you start

You need User Management in your left-hand menu. If it isn't there, your own login doesn't have admin rights — ask someone who does.

PART 1

Create the user

User Management › Users

  1. Click + New User at the top right.

  2. Fill in the details:

    FieldWhat to enter
    User Code * A short staff code. Follow whatever the last user used — for example SYN-017.
    Username * What they type to log in. House style is first name, dot, surname — siju.nagrajan. It can't be changed later without confusing their history, so get it right now.
    Full Name Their real name. This is what everyone else sees on approvals and documents.
    Email Needed if they should receive approval or alert emails. Leave blank if not.
    Mobile Optional.
    Password * A starting password, typed twice. They should change it after their first login.

  3. Leave Active ticked, and Locked unticked.

    Untick Active later when someone leaves — that keeps their name on old documents while stopping them logging in. Deleting the user does not.

  4. Save.

They can log in now, but they'll see nothing. A brand new user has no role, and the menu is built entirely from roles. Carry straight on to Part 2.
PART 2

Give the user a role

User Management › Users  ·  or   Assign Roles

A role is the job someone does — Procurement Officer, Finance Manager, Engineer. It decides which screens they get. Two routes to the same result:

  1. From the user's row. On Users, find the person and click Roles on their line. Tick the role or roles they need, and save.

  2. Or from the Assign Roles screen. Go to User Management › Assign Roles, search for the person by name, tick their roles, and save. Easier when you're setting up several people in one go.

More than one role

Someone can hold several roles at once — they get everything all of those roles allow, added together. Give the smallest set that lets them do their job.

Ask them to log out and back in. The menu is worked out at login, so a role added while they're signed in won't appear until they sign in again.
PART 3

Set what a role can do

User Management › Role Permissions

Permissions belong to the role, not the person. Change them once and everyone holding that role is updated. If you're adding someone to a role that already exists and works, you can stop here — this part is only for new roles, or when an existing role needs adjusting.

  1. Pick the role from the list at the top. Until you do, the page just says Select a role above to configure its permissions.

  2. Tick the screens that role should reach. They're grouped the same way as the left-hand menu — Job Costing, Procurement, Inventory, Finance and so on — so you can open a group and tick the screens inside it.

  3. For each screen, choose what they may do on it:

    • VIEWOpen the screen and read it
    • ADDCreate new records
    • EDITChange existing records
    • DELETERemove records
    • PRINTPrint or save as PDF
    • APPROVEApprove or reject documents
    • EXPORTDownload to Excel

    VIEW is the one that matters most: without it the screen doesn't appear in their menu at all, no matter what else is ticked.

  4. Save.

Approvals are separate. Ticking APPROVE lets someone use the approve buttons, but it does not put documents in front of them. Who approves what, and in which order, is set up under approval policies — a different job from this one.
IF NEEDED

Creating a brand new role

User Management › Roles

  1. Click + New Role.

  2. Enter a Role Name people will recognise — for example Finance Manager — a short Role Code such as FIN_MGR, and a description of what the job covers.

  3. Save, then go to Role Permissions and give it its screens, as in Part 3.

Check first

Roles are easier to run when there are fewer of them. Before adding one, look at whether an existing role already covers the job.

IF STUCK

Common problems

They log in, but the menu is empty.
No role, or a role with no screens ticked. Check Part 2, then Part 3.
They can open a screen but the buttons are missing.
The role has VIEW but not ADD or EDIT on that screen. Add the rights they need in Role Permissions.
Nothing changed after you gave them a role.
They're still on their old session. Have them log out and log back in.
They've forgotten their password.
On Users, click Reset Pwd on their row and set a new one. Tell them to change it after logging in.
They're locked out after too many wrong passwords.
Open the user and untick Locked, then save.
Someone has left the company.
Untick Active on their record. Don't delete them — their name needs to stay on the jobs, orders and approvals they handled.
