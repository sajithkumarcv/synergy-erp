Getting Started

This manual walks you through the everyday tasks in the ERP — creating master records, running the procurement cycle, sending documents through approval, and raising invoices and vouchers. Screens follow the same pattern throughout, so once you learn one, the rest feel familiar.

What you see & what you can do depends on your role The menus in the left panel, and the action buttons on every screen — Add, Edit, Approve, Print, Delete, Revise — appear only if your assigned role grants them. Two people can open the same document and see different buttons. If a menu or button you expect is missing, your role simply doesn't include it — ask your administrator.
No role, no access If your account has no role assigned (or a role with no screens), sign-in is refused with a clear message. You must be given a role before you can use the system.

1.1 Signing in

  1. Open the application URL. A branded sign-in screen appears for your company.
  2. Enter your Username and Password, then click Sign In.
  3. Forgot your password? Click Forgot password?, enter your username and email, and a reset link is sent to you.
One session at a time Each account can be signed in on one PC at a time. If you see “Already signed in on another PC”, continuing here will sign the other one out. Your session ends when you close the browser.

1.2 Navigating the app

  • Left menu — groups every module (Masters, Procurement, Finance, Inventory, Settings). Click a module to open its list.
  • List screen — search, filter and page through records; click a row to open its detail page.
  • Detail page — a header summary (KPI strip) on top, then tabs (Overview, Lines, Documents, Approval, …). Action buttons (Edit, Submit, Print, Delete) sit in the top-right.

1.3 How a document moves — its lifecycle

Most business documents (PR, PO, GRN, Invoice, Vouchers) follow the same lifecycle. The status badge on the document always tells you where it is.

Master Data

Masters are the reference records — customers, suppliers and items — that every transaction pulls from. Set them up once; reuse them everywhere.

2.1 Create a Customer

Masters ▸ Customers

  1. Open Customers and click + New Customer.
  2. Enter the core details: Customer Name, code, Currency, Payment Terms, Credit Limit / Credit Days, and the VAT / TRN number.
  3. Save. The customer opens on its detail page with tabs.
  4. Open the Contacts tab → add contact persons (name, designation, phone, email). Mark one as primary.
  5. Open the Addresses tab → add one or more addresses (Billing, Shipping…). Set the Default address — it is what appears on invoices.
Credit hold A customer can be placed on Credit Hold. While on hold, new invoices for that customer cannot be approved until the hold is cleared.

2.2 Create a Supplier

Masters ▸ Suppliers

  1. Open Suppliers → + New Supplier.
  2. Enter name, code, currency, payment/delivery terms and tax number.
  3. Add Contacts and Addresses on their tabs, exactly like a customer.

Suppliers you create here become selectable on Purchase Orders and supplier invoices.

2.3 Create an Item

Masters ▸ Items

  1. Open Items → + New Item.
  2. Enter Item Code and Item Name.
  3. Set the item's units of measure:
    • Base UOM — the unit stock is held and valued in (e.g. PCS).
    • Purchase UOM — the unit you buy in on a PO (e.g. BOX).
    • Sales UOM — the unit you sell / invoice in.
  4. Set whether the item is Stockable (tracked in inventory) and, if used, its budget / cost category.
  5. Save. The item is now available on requisitions, orders and receipts.

UOM conversion — buy in one unit, stock in another

When an item is purchased in a different unit from how it is stored — bought by the BOX but issued by the PIECE — define a UOM conversion so the system converts quantities automatically. No manual maths, and stock stays correct in the base unit.

  1. Open the item and go to its UOM Conversions.
  2. Add a conversion with From UOM, To UOM and a Conversion Factor. Example — From BOX → To PCS, Factor 12 means 1 box = 12 pieces.
  3. Save. Now a PO for 5 BOX receives as 60 PCS into stock, and costing, balances and issues all stay right in the base unit.
Why it matters Conversions keep purchase, stock and sales units consistent — you order and receive in the supplier's unit while inventory, budget and issues all work in the base unit.

Bulk import items from Excel

To load many items at once instead of adding them one by one:

  1. On the Items list, choose Import. The screen shows the required column layout (download the template if one is offered).
  2. Fill one row per item — Item Code, Item Name, Base UOM, Purchase UOM, Sales UOM, stockable flag, category, etc. UOMs may be entered by their code or name.
  3. Upload the file. Each row is validated — good rows are imported, and any row with a problem (missing name, unknown UOM…) is listed with the reason, so you fix and re-upload just those.
Before you import The UOMs you reference must already exist in the system — an unknown UOM code / name makes that row fail. Item Code and Base UOM are required on every row.

Project & Procurement Cycle

For a project (customer) job, buying never starts with a purchase order. It starts with the Job, then its Budget, then its BOM — each approved in turn. Only after the BOM is approved does PR → PO → GRN open up.

Follow the sequence — it is enforced You cannot raise a Purchase Requisition until the job's Budget and BOM are approved. Work through the steps below in order.
StepDo thisYou reach
1Create the Job and fill all its details, then submitJob Approved
2Create the Budget (cost categories & amounts), then submitBudget Approved
3Create the BOM from the approved budget, then submitBOM Approved
4Raise a PR → convert to PO → receive with a GRNMaterial received
In-house jobs — still tied to a budget header The full Job → Budget → BOM → PR → PO → GRN path applies to project / customer jobs. In-house jobs follow a shorter path (not gated by BOM approval), but each in-house job is still linked to a budget header: you may only purchase items that fall under that job's budget. That link is what lets the system track every item bought and consumed against the job, and keeps spend inside the approved budget.

3.1 Create a Job

Jobs ▸ Jobs

  1. Open Jobs → + New Job.
  2. Fill the details: job name / number, Customer, description, Job Type (this scopes the BOM later), currency & exchange rate, dates and any LPO / reference.
  3. Save as Draft, then Submit for Approval. Complete every mandatory field — the system validates them before the job can enter the workflow.
  4. Once Approved, the job is live and you can create its budget.

3.2 Job Budget

Jobs ▸ (open job) ▸ Budget

  1. Open the approved job and go to its Budget.
  2. Add a budget line per cost / expense category with its budgeted amount (and currency / rate). This is the ceiling every future purchase order is checked against.
  3. Submit the Budget for approval. Approving the budget is what lets you build the BOM.
Changing an item later? Start from the budget To add, remove or change a material after the cycle has begun, do not edit the PR or PO directly. Go back and revise the Budget first (revise → re-approve), then update the BOM, and only then the procurement documents — so budget, BOM and purchases stay in step.

3.3 Bill of Materials (BOM)

BOM ▸ Bill of Materials

  1. Create a BOM for the approved job. Its sections and items are scoped to the job's Job Type.
  2. Add the materials and quantities the job needs — the BOM can be generated from the approved budget.
  3. Submit the BOM for approval. Once Approved, procurement opens up.

Procurement — PR → PO → GRN

Purchase Requisition

“We need these items.” Raised for the job against its approved BOM / budget.

Purchase Order

“Ordered from this supplier.” Sent to the vendor after approval.

Goods Receipt (GRN)

“Received into stock.” Records what actually arrived against the PO.

3.4 Purchase Requisition (PR)

Procurement ▸ Purchase Requests

  1. Open Purchase Requests → + New PR.
  2. Select the Job the requisition is for, and fill the header (required date, priority).
  3. On the Lines tab, add each item: pick the Item, enter Quantity, UOM and the budget/expense category.
  4. Save as Draft, then click Submit for Approval.
  5. Once fully Approved, the procurement team is notified and can raise a PO from it.

3.5 Purchase Order (PO)

Procurement ▸ Purchase Orders

  1. Open Purchase Orders → + New PO (or create one directly from an approved PR).
  2. Header: choose the Supplier, Currency (exchange rate auto-fills), Payment Terms, Delivery Terms and the Job.
  3. On Lines, add items with Ordered Qty and Unit Price. Totals (Subtotal, VAT, Discount, Total) calculate automatically.
  4. Add Terms & Conditions and any annexures if your format uses them.
  5. Submit for Approval. Approval checks the budget for the job/category and may block or require an override.
  6. When Approved, Print the PO (see §7) and send it to the supplier.
POs are linked to the budget header — spend is controlled Every PO line is tied to the job's budget header (expense category). On submit, the system totals all committed POs for that category and blocks the PO if it would exceed the approved budget — so costing stays under control and the budget cannot overflow. Only an authorised approver can override with the budget password (§4), and the reason is logged.
Revisions Need to change an approved PO? Use Revise. This creates a new revision (Rev.1, Rev.2 …) that goes through approval again, keeping a full history.

3.6 Goods Receipt Note (GRN)

Procurement ▸ GRN

  1. Open GRN → + New GRN and select the Purchase Order being received.
  2. The PO lines load. Enter the Received Quantity for each (partial receipts are allowed — the balance stays open on the PO).
  3. Record delivery note / invoice references and the receiving store/location.
  4. Save and Submit. On approval, stock for stockable items is increased and the PO's received quantities update.

3.7 Service Receipt & Free-Issue GRN

  • Service Receipt (SRV) — confirms that a service on a PO was delivered (no stock movement). Procurement ▸ Service Receipt
  • Free-Issue GRN — receives material provided free by the customer/principal for a job, tracked separately from purchased stock. Procurement ▸ Free-Issue GRN

Approvals

Approval is the same across every document. When you submit, the system routes it through the approval policy that matches the document and its amount.

Submitting

On a Draft document, click Submit for Approval. The status becomes Pending at Level 1 and the assigned approver is notified.

Acting on a document (approvers)

Open the document's Approval tab, or find it under Approvals ▸ My Approvals. Choose an action and add remarks:

ActionEffect
ApproveMoves to the next level, or marks the document Approved/Confirmed at the final level. Requires your login password.
Send BackReturns the document to the previous level (or to the submitter as a draft) for correction — Sent Back.
RejectDeclines the document — Rejected. It cannot proceed.
CancelWithdraws the document (submitter) — Cancelled.
Delegates Going on leave? An administrator can set a delegate for a date range, so your pending approvals route to a colleague automatically. The trail records who acted and on whose behalf.
Budget override If a PO would exceed the job's budget for an expense category, approval is blocked. An authorised approver can enter the budget password to override, and the reason is logged.

Finance

Bill customers, record money received and paid, and issue credit or debit notes — all sharing the same create → approve → print rhythm.

5.1 Tax Invoice

Finance ▸ Invoices

  1. Open Invoices → + New Invoice.
  2. Select the Customer (billing address, contact and currency pre-fill), the Job, and the LPO / PO No.
  3. On Lines, add each line: description, quantity, unit price and VAT %. Sub-total, VAT and Total calculate automatically; the amount is also shown in words.
  4. Add any Notes / Remarks and save as Draft.
  5. Submit for Approval. Once Confirmed, Print the tax invoice (§7).
Editing after approval A confirmed invoice is locked. Use Revise to make a corrected revision (requires a reason and, if configured, a password), which re-enters approval.

5.2 Receipt Voucher (money in)

Finance ▸ Receipt Vouchers

  1. Open Receipt Vouchers → + New.
  2. Select the Customer, the amount received, currency, payment mode (cash / cheque / transfer) and reference.
  3. Allocate the amount against the customer's open invoices (fully or partially). Any unallocated balance is shown.
  4. Submit for approval; on approval the invoices' outstanding balances reduce.

5.3 Payment Voucher (money out)

Finance ▸ Payment Vouchers

The mirror of a receipt voucher: record a payment to a supplier, choose the bank/payment mode, and allocate it against the supplier's invoices. Submit for approval as usual.

5.4 Credit & Debit Notes

  • Credit Note — reduces what a customer owes (returns, corrections). Finance ▸ Credit Notes
  • Debit Note — raises a charge against a supplier. Finance ▸ Debit Notes

Both are created, allocated against invoices, and approved the same way as vouchers.

Manhours & Timesheets

Record labour hours against jobs and send them for approval — they feed job costing and analysis.

  1. Download the template from Manhours ▸ Manhour Upload.
  2. Fill it in — employee, job, date and hours for the period.
  3. Upload the completed file. The system validates the rows and creates a manhour batch.
  4. Submit for approval. Once Approved, the hours are costed to the job using each employee's manhour rate.
ScreenUse it to…
Manhour UploadDownload the template, upload filled timesheets, and submit the batch for approval.
Manhour RatesMaintain the hourly cost rate per employee / category used to cost the hours.
EmployeesThe employee master used on timesheets.
Budget-controlled, like POs Manhour cost is also checked against the job budget; an authorised approver can override with the budget password when necessary.

Printing Documents

  1. Open any document (Invoice, PO, GRN, Voucher…) and click Print in the top-right.
  2. A print preview opens in the selected layout. Click Print / Save as PDF.
  3. In the browser dialog, choose your printer — or “Save as PDF” to keep an electronic copy.
One layout per document Which print layout each document uses is set centrally by an administrator (see §8), so every user prints the same, approved design. A Draft document prints with a “PREVIEW / DRAFT” watermark until it is approved.
